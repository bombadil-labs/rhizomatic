Rhizomatic Engineering · the live example

Two tools, a mapping and a policy

Two tools record a release decision under different field names. A third person publishes a mapping between the names. A reader chooses whether to accept that mapping, and which policy decides. The argument for building software this way is on the front page.

This runs the real implementation in your tab. No server.

What just happened

  
    
    
  1. Two tools, no shared schema. One says status, the other says verdict. Each adapter signs one claim about the same build.
    2. 
    
  2. A mapping is a claim. Sam's mapping claims carry later timestamps. Without them, the gate does not match the verdict claim.
    3. 
    
  3. The reader decides. The policy picks one claim. The page names the claim that decided. Without Sam's mapping, both policies answer ship. With it, the timestamp policy answers block because support's claim has the higher claimed timestamp. Engineering's word wins still answers ship.
    4. 
    
  4. A third tool needed nobody's permission. The report reads only the pack of published claims.
